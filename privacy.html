Privacy policy

This policy describes how this website handles information when you browse the portfolio or contact ThINK Tattoo.

Information you choose to share

The inquiry form prepares a draft in your own email application. Form contents are not sent to a website server or stored by this website. If you send the email, Robb receives the information you include, such as your name, email address, tattoo idea, and any attachments, to respond to your inquiry and discuss your appointment.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts and portfolio images are served with the website. The location section embeds Google Maps, which connects to Google when loaded and may process technical information or use cookies under Google’s privacy policy. A hosting provider may process basic technical request information to deliver the site.

External services

Links to Facebook and Google Maps open third-party services. Your email provider also handles messages you send. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Your information

Send only information needed to discuss your tattoo. Avoid sending sensitive medical information through the inquiry form. Contact Robb to ask about information you have shared or to request its correction or deletion, subject to any applicable recordkeeping requirements.
