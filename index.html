05 / BEFORE YOU SIT DOWN

A FEW
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Something else on your mind?
Call Robb: (760) 912-0549

How do I book a tattoo? +

Call, email, or message Robb on Facebook with your idea, placement, approximate size, and reference photos. Robb will discuss the next steps and confirm availability directly.

How much will my tattoo cost? +

Ask Robb for a quote based on the design, size, placement, and time required. Confirm current rates and any deposit or cancellation terms before booking.

Can I bring my own references? +

Yes. Bring images that explain the style, subject, or feeling you have in mind. They’re a starting point for a conversation about your own piece.

Can you rework an existing tattoo? +

Send a clear photo of the current tattoo and explain the change you’re hoping for. Robb will assess whether a rework or cover-up is suitable.

Where is the studio? +

40555 California Oaks Road, Suite 131, Murrieta, California. Contact Robb to confirm your appointment and arrival details. Welcoming clients from Menifee, Temecula, Wildomar, and Lake Elsinore.

How should I prepare? +

Ask Robb for preparation instructions when you book. Discuss any relevant skin sensitivities or health concerns with your artist and a qualified healthcare professional. Follow the aftercare instructions provided at your appointment.
